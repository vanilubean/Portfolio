About Me

Hi, I'm Jerica — but you can call me Ria. I'm an artist, developer, and designer from the Philippines who believes that code can be beautiful and art can be functional.

I taught myself to draw, but I went to school for Computer Science at Mindanao State University - Maigo. That's where I learned to bridge the gap between imagination and execution.

Today, I fuse illustration, interaction design, and development to create digital experiences that feel alive. I bring the same mindset: make it meaningful, make it memorable.
